## Focus on patent law

Is the gender gap a **specific problem** of **patent law**?
There have been some studies that have shown that women **do receive unequal treatment**, namely they are **more likely to be rejected** by about $8.2\%$ than those of men.

>[!note] Approaches for change?
>There are some linguistic definitions in patent law that were clearly designed with **men** in mind, those need to be rewritten. (Fachmann being used in old text instead of Fachperson)
>
>A new **form of protection**, that allows **any invention** to be protected, for a short period of time, even though the patent has not been accepted. (this one doesn't sound good ngl)
>
>Other measures **specifically targeted** towards women (helping, motivating more women to join the profession of IP, creation of support groups, ...)
