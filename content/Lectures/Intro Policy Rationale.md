# Big picture and definition

>[!example] Big picture
>![[Pasted image 20260224122840.png]]

If we wanted to really define **IP**, we could say that:

- @ It's a **legal concept**
- @ Provides **exclusive rights** for *creations of the mind* (inventions, literature, musical works, designs, etc.)
- @ It's a form of **ownership**: we have the right to exclude others from using it
- @ Protects **tangible** as well as **intangible** assets (as we've seen before with NFT's)
