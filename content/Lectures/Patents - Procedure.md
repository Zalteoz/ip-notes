>[!danger] The danger of oppositions
>If someone opposes your patent after it's been granted in **EPO** (in the first 9months after the grant), you might lose the case (for example if you sue the opposition and you lose the case, or if the grant is actually revoked after further inspection), you might lose **very big**, imagine that knowing you had been granted the patent, you made some deals with some companies for **licensing**, now those are not liable to any payment to you and they're free to use your invention, since you lost your patent, you can't even sue them.
>
>You're **not safe** before the opposition period *expires*, so take that into consideration before making too many licensing deals
